Kewei Zhang 张科伟

kwzhang at bnu dot edu dot cn  |  kwzhang at pku dot edu dot cn

I am a professor at Beijing Normal University.

I was a postdoc at BICMR during 2019-2021.

I completed my Ph.D. in 2019 at Peking University, China.

I was also a visiting student at the University of Maryland during 2017-2018.

My research interests include complex geometry, algebraic geometry, and geometric PDE.
